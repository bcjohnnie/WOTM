What Congress Should Be Reading website tracks newly released Congressional Research Service (CRS) reports and related materials, providing plain-language summaries, context, and analysis to help the public better understand the information prepared for Congress. CRS reports are produced by the nonpartisan research arm of the Library of Congress to inform Members of Congress and their staff. Although CRS materials have been publicly available since 2018, it remains difficult for the general public to identify newly released reports or understand their significance without sustained monitoring. This publication helps address that gap by regularly highlighting newly issued or updated reports and offering concise synopses so readers can quickly identify topics relevant to their interests.
