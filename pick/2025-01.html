H2O : build a better casebook

The resource at its current address.

H2O is a free platform that helps faculty reduce textbook costs for their students, gain greater control over their course materials, and expand the possibilities of open legal education. The open-access casebooks in H2O enable users to create, share, and remix openly licensed casebooks and other course materials. The platform is developed and maintained by the Library Innovation Lab at the Harvard Law School Library. More detailed information can be found here.
