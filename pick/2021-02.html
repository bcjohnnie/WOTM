Justia is a legal website specializing in information retrieval on law and provides free access to case law, codes, regulations and legal information for lawyers, business, students and consumers world wide in addition to opinion summaries, and other basic legal texts, with paid services for its attorney directory and webhosting. It was founded in 2003 and is one of the largest online databases of legal cases. Justia US law provides the full text of the Annotated US Constitution, as well as recent dockets and selected case filings from the US federal district and appellate courts. In 2007, the New York Times reported that Justia was spending around “$10,000 a month” in order “to copy documents” from the United States Supreme Court and publish them online, to be made available without the public paying fees.
