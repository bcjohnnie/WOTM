Regulations.gov is a U. S. Federal government Website that allows users to make public comments in response to notices of proposed rulemaking issued by participating agencies; such comments become part of the public record and may be displayed on the site. From the Regulations.gov, you can view a description of every proposed and final Federal regulation currently open for comment, read the full text of the regulations, and submit your comments to the Federal agencies responsible for the rule making action. Searches by government agency name or by keyword are available
