Copyright Public Records Portal

The resource at its current address.

The Copyright Public Records Portal is the online search system provided by the United States Copyright Office that allows the public to search copyright registration and recordation records maintained by the U.S. Copyright Office. The portal allows users to: - Search copyright registration records. - Search recorded documents, such as copyright transfers, assignments, and licenses. - Locate information about copyright owners, authors, claimants, titles, and registration numbers. - Access newer records through a modern search interface For librarians and legal researchers, the portal serves as a starting point for identifying copyright registrations and ownership information maintained by the U.S. Copyright Office. It is particularly useful when researching the copyright status of published works or tracing ownership through recorded transfers.
