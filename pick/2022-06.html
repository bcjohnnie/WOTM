The Court Listener Website provides public access to primary legal materials on the Internet for educational, charitable, and scientific purposes to the benefit of the general public and the public interest as well as to technologies useful for academic research on corpora and legal systems. Free Law Project is a California non-profit public benefit corporation and a federally-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity whose specific purposes are primarily to create an open ecosystem for legal research and materials and to carry on other charitable activities associated with these purposes, including, but not limited to, publications, meetings, conferences, trainings, educational seminars, and the issuance of grants and other financial support to educational institutions, foundations, and other organizations exclusively for educational, charitable, and scientific purposes as allowed by law. Free Law Project also has several initiatives that collect and share legal information, including the largest collection of American oral argument audio, daily collection of new legal opinions from 200 United States courts and administrative bodies.
