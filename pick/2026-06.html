Travel.state.gov is a website created by the U.S. Department of State, Bureau of Consular Affairs, which provides practical information for various topics that affect American citizens who are dealing with legal and other issues aboard and legal resources and judicial assistance information such as how to find an attorney in the foreign jurisdiction.
