Non-Human Rights Database

This database compiles legislation and case law from international and local jurisdictions that recognize rights for non-human entities, including animals, rivers, ecosystems, AI, and machines. It also includes cases in which non-human entities have been granted special legal status, even when full legal rights have not been formally recognized. The database is organized by resource type (legislation and judicial decisions) as well as by jurisdiction (international and local). These categories are further subdivided to facilitate ease of use and navigation. Most resources are presented in their original language, and English translations are provided whenever available. When original legislation, case law, or project documents cannot be obtained, alternative sources-such as press articles-are included to provide relevant context. Although still a work in progress, the database aspires to serve as an expanding resource for legal professionals, scholars, and activists engaged in the emerging field of non-human rights.
