About this collection

The LSRD-SIS Legal Website of the Month began on the section's e-list in 2000/01. The LSRD-SIS's own archive pages list each pick under a year heading (the earliest say 1997, though those headings run several years early) with a title, a short description and, in early years, a catalogue link. This site republishes that list as a link collection: one page per pick, connected to every other pick by topic, jurisdiction, resource type, publisher and Library of Congress subject heading.

Who did what

Values gathered from existing sources are kept apart from work Claude (Anthropic) did for this project. The two are published as separate archives that join on Entry ID: the compiled archive (gathered data and verified links only) and the LLM-assisted archive (Claude's work). This site draws on both, and every value carries a badge saying which.

Preserved from original sources LSRD-SIS Library catalogue

  • Descriptions, verbatim from the LSRD-SIS archive: 343 of 344 picks.
  • LSRD year headings and archive pages, as published: 344.
  • Titles as LSRD listed them. They are kept in the MARC (246) and shown here converted from capitals.
  • Subject headings (LCSH) copied from law-library catalogue records: 203 picks (113 University of Hawaiʻi, 87 Colorado, 3 from the 2017 LSRD MARC file).
  • Publisher from the University of Tennessee Law mirror's record or a catalogue record: 71 picks.
  • OCLC numbers from catalogue records: 128 from Hawaiʻi or the 2017 file, 85 from the Colorado record LSRD linked.
  • Original addresses as catalogued or recorded when the site was featured, where they still verify: 246. Some of these were located by LLM agents, as listed on the right.
  • Archived copies from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine, and authority URIs from the Library of Congress.
  • Access notes recorded by the project owner: 21 picks Owner.

Work done by Claude LLM-assisted Machine-assigned

  • Titles converted from capitals, and some corrected from catalogue records: all 344.
  • Topic, jurisdiction, place and resource type for all 344 picks, from the LSRD description or catalogue headings. The topic list was reviewed by the owner.
  • Subject headings machine-assigned where no catalogue gave any: 134 picks. Each heading was checked against id.loc.gov, but none was reviewed by a cataloguer.
  • Publisher / issuing body as LC authorized names (MARC 710/700), machine-assigned and checked against id.loc.gov: 241 picks.
  • Access (Free or Subscription), judged from the site: 316 picks.
  • Estimated posting months for all 344, from dated archive sources, because the year headings run early.
  • Notes on errors in LSRD entries: 7.
  • Values found by LLM agents and then identity-checked, in 122 picks: current address (46), original address (40), WorldCat OCLC number (32), equivalent 1 (19), status wording (19), archived copy (19). The OCLC numbers found this way (30 in this collection) were confirmed by the owner. Each pick's page names its own.
  • The MARC records, generated from the data above (minimal-level, RDA).
  • The tooling: the link checks, the identity verification of every link, the status checks, and this website. Links the scripts could not settle went to the owner for review.

Not done by anyone: no LSRD description has been rewritten or summarised, and no link was added without an identity check.
